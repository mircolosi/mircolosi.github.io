About Me

Robotics & Computer Vision Engineer based in Zurich, Switzerland

A Bit About Me

I am a Senior Vision & Perception Engineer at NEURA Robotics in Zurich, Switzerland, with a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Robotics from Sapienza University of Rome (conducted in collaboration with Robert Bosch GmbH).

With extensive industry and research experience spanning Bosch Corporate Research and NEURA Robotics, I specialize in vSLAM, 3D Gaussian Splatting, Humanoid State Estimation (InvEKF), cross-sensor calibration, and modular real-time perception for autonomous platforms.

Passionate about coding, AI, and cutting-edge challenges. Love to travel, DIY, and videogames. Active open-source contributor in the ROS2 and robotics community.
